ABOUT US

A Place for Good Food

Rede Alimentar provides Angolan and international dishes in a comfortable and welcoming environment.

Located in Kinaxixi, Luanda, the restaurant welcomes families, local residents, tourists, business professionals and students.

We also provide takeaway services and catering for special events.
